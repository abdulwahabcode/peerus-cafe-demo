Our Story

Where Lahore comes to gather.

Peeru's Café began with a simple idea: a place where good food and good art share the same table. Over the years it has grown into Lahore's beloved arts & culture café — poets, musicians, students and families, all under one warm roof.

By day, the tandoor glows and the chai never stops. By night, the courtyard fills with the sound of the harmonium and tabla as our resident qawwals take the stage.

“Friday nights here are not just dinner — they are a mehfil. You come for the parathas, you stay for the qawwali.” — A regular since 2014
